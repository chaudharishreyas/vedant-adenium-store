A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY

Sunshine, patience
and a little love.

01Bright sunlight

Adeniums love plenty of light. Introduce direct sun gradually.

02Water thoughtfully

Let the growing medium dry between waterings; avoid waterlogging.

03Well-draining soil

Choose a free-draining mix and a pot with drainage holes.

Care needs vary with climate, season, pot and plant size.
